NIGHT FLIGHTS

When a service falls over, the plane scrambles

UptimeRobot mails that a monitor is down; Preflight flies it across your screen with a beacon and a two-tone siren. Red and amber, flashing well under three times a second — we read the WCAG so you don't have to. Meetings stay silent. Outages do not.

NEW IN 0.3.0

When a deployment fails, the plane says Fahhh

Azure DevOps mails that a release stage failed — usually an E2E run that wants another go. Preflight flies it across with the beacon lit and the stage's name on the banner, so you know which one before you have even looked up.

Click the banner to rerun

It opens that very deployment in Azure DevOps — the link out of the mail, minus the tracking gunk. Rerun it and get on with your day.

Not the siren

An outage gets the two-tone siren. A failed deployment gets a five-second clip that says exactly what you were about to say.

Your call, twice over

Each alert has its own switch in Settings, and so does each sound. One volume covers both. Meetings stay silent, as ever.

Cleared for takeoff in five steps

Download the DMG above

Apple Silicon or Intel — pick your fuselage.

Drag Preflight into Applications

Standard procedure. Nothing exciting happens yet.

Clear security

macOS will claim the app is damaged. It is not — macOS says that about any plane without papers (an Apple Developer certificate). One command in Terminal clears it, once, forever:

xattr -cr /Applications/Preflight.app

Rather not use Terminal? Right-click the app → Open → Open again. Recent macOS may also want System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Open it, find the rabbit

Preflight lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Look for the rabbit. It shows your next meeting right in the menu.

Connect Google Calendar

Press Connect and sign in. From then on, every meeting gets a flyby. Try Test flight from the menu to show a colleague immediately.

QUESTIONS FROM THE CABIN

Frequently asked, honestly answered

Does it read my calendar?

Titles, times, attendees and meeting links for the next 90 minutes, on your Mac only. The Google access is read-only — it could not change your calendar even if the rabbit wanted to. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Does it read my mail?

Only to spot two kinds of alert: UptimeRobot saying a monitor is down, and Azure DevOps saying a deployment failed. It reads the subject lines of those, and opens the body of a failed-deployment mail for one thing — the link to the deployment. Nothing else in your inbox is ever opened, and nothing leaves your Mac.

I have two monitors. Which one does it fly on?

All of them, by default — a reminder on the screen you are not looking at is not a reminder. Presenting? Switch to "Main screen only" in Settings and it stays off the projector.

I find it distracting.

That is broadly the point. But Settings can mute all-day events, declined meetings, free blocks and solo focus time — or set a single 1-minute warning for maximum adrenaline.

Why is the rabbit flying the plane?

Someone has to.
